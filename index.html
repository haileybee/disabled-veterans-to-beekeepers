Our Story

Meet Donald Schafer

Retired AO2 Petty Officer Donald Schafer is an eight-year disabled Navy veteran who served our country as an Aviation Ordnanceman.

Don is one of five Schafer brothers who joined the Navy. It is highly unusual for all siblings to serve at the same time, let alone in the same branch of service. Each brother’s name is inscribed, one after another, on the Wall of Honor in their hometown of Escanaba, Michigan.

After returning home, Don found himself searching for a meaningful way to apply the skills and sense of purpose he developed during his military service.

The Spark

A story from 1935 helped inspire a new future.

Don’s inspiration came from the 1935 film Keeper of the Bees, about a World War I veteran who became a beekeeper and passed his knowledge from one generation to another. That idea stayed with him: veterans carrying hard-earned discipline, resilience, and service into a peaceful new calling.

Rooted in Michigan’s Upper Peninsula

Don eventually moved to a 20-acre farm outside Escanaba, where honeybees became part of a larger vision for service, healing, and teaching.

Why Beekeeping?

Quiet work. Clear purpose. A living system that rewards care.

Beekeeping asks for patience, observation, consistency, and respect for the natural world. Those qualities can turn a hive into more than an agricultural project. It can become a place to rebuild confidence and direction.
