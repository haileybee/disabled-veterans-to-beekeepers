About Don

Retired AO2 Petty Officer Donald Schafer is an eight-year disabled Navy veteran who served our country as an Aviation Ordnanceman.

After returning home, Don found himself searching for a meaningful way to apply the skills and sense of purpose he developed during his military service. Beekeeping became a new mission rooted in patience, purpose, and passing knowledge forward.

Read Don's Story 🐝

From Service to a New Mission

Don is one of five Schafer brothers who joined the Navy. Each brother’s name is inscribed on the Wall of Honor in their hometown of Escanaba, Michigan.

His inspiration came from the 1935 film Keeper of the Bees, about a World War I veteran who became a beekeeper and passed his knowledge from one generation to another. Don eventually moved to a 20-acre farm outside Escanaba, where honeybees became part of a larger vision for service, healing, and teaching.
