A Family of Service

The Schafer Brothers

Five brothers, one Navy tradition, and a story of service that became part of Don's next mission.

From Service to
A New Mission

Don is one of five Schafer brothers who joined the Navy. Each brother’s name is inscribed on the Wall of Honor in their hometown of Escanaba, Michigan.

His inspiration came from the 1935 film Keeper of the Bees, about a World War I veteran who became a beekeeper and passed his knowledge from one generation to another.

Don eventually moved to a 20-acre farm outside Escanaba, where honeybees became part of a larger vision for service, healing, and teaching.
